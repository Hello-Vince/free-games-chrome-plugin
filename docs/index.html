Catch free games before they’re gone.

Free Games Tracker brings active GamerPower giveaways into a focused Chrome popup, with platform filters, local visited-offer tracking, and an unseen-offer badge.

No accounts, analytics, advertising, or extension-owned backend. Preferences and visited-offer history stay in Chrome’s local extension storage.
